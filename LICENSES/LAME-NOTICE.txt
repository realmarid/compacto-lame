LAME (MP3 encoder)

Compacto uses LAME 3.100 to make MP3 files from video or audio.

LAME is open-source software distributed under the GNU Library General Public License, version 2 (LGPL). The full license text is in src/main/cpp/lame/COPYING.

Compacto uses LAME unmodified, as a shared library (.so) kept separate from the app's own code.

The LAME source included in Compacto (an unmodified subset of lame-3.100) and the files used to build the library (the CMake configuration and the JNI bridge) are in this repository under src/main/cpp/. The complete original (lame-3.100.tar.gz) is also at https://lame.sourceforge.io/. For at least three years from the date we distribute this app, you can also request them by email.

You may rebuild this library (libcompactolame.so) from modified LAME source and replace it; reverse engineering to debug such modifications is permitted.
